About Us

Built for Everyone Who Works with Images


Aris Image Pixels was created out of a simple frustration: most online image tools are cluttered with ads, require you to create an account, and upload your photos to a remote server — meaning your private images are no longer private.

We built Aris Image Pixels to be different. Every operation happens entirely inside your web browser using the Canvas API. Your files never leave your device. There are no accounts to create, no email addresses to provide, and no hidden limits on how many images you can process.

Our mission is to give everyone — from professional photographers and designers to students and small business owners — a free, fast, and trustworthy tool for everyday image editing tasks. Whether you need to resize a product photo, compress an image for a website, or convert a file to a different format, Aris Image Pixels has you covered.

Aris Image Pixels is a proud sub-brand of Aris Studio — a creative studio dedicated to building accessible, elegant digital tools for everyone. We are a small, passionate team committed to quality and simplicity.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions


Is Aris Image Pixels completely free to use?

Yes, Aris Image Pixels is 100% free. There are no hidden fees, no subscriptions, and no sign-up required. You can resize, compress, and convert unlimited images at no cost.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Absolutely not. Every operation happens entirely within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript. Your images never leave your device — they are never sent to any server, stored, or shared with anyone.

What image formats does Aris Image Pixels support?

You can upload JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF images. For output, you can choose between JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. WebP is recommended for the best quality-to-size ratio for web use.

What does DPI mean and why does it matter?

DPI stands for Dots Per Inch — it measures how many pixels are packed into each inch of an image. For screen use, 72–96 DPI is standard. For professional print work, you typically need 300 DPI to ensure sharp, crisp output. Our tool lets you set any DPI value between 1 and 600.

Can I use this tool on my phone or tablet?

Yes! Aris Image Pixels is fully responsive and works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Is there a file size or image dimension limit?

There is no imposed limit by our tool — the limit is your device's memory. Very large images (above 50 MP) may be slow on older devices. For most everyday images, processing is near-instant.
